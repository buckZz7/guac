# guac — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [Date]. This is a plain-English explanation. It is not legal
advice._

guac ("we", "us") runs an OpenAI-compatible inference gateway. This policy
explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it. By using guac you
consent to the practices described here.

## 1. What we collect and why

- **Account identity.** When you sign up (user or advertiser) we store your
  email address and an API key / advertiser token. Needed to operate your
  account.
- **Inference requests.** Prompts and model outputs are **forwarded to
  third-party inference providers** (e.g. OpenRouter, Chutes, engy) to generate
  responses. This is the core function of the service.
- **Usage ledger.** We record per-request token counts, supplier used, sponsor
  shown, and impression/click attribution. Needed to meter, bill, and settle
  transparently.
- **Payments.** When payments are enabled, payment details are processed by our
  payment provider (e.g. Stripe). We do not store full card numbers; those are
  handled by the provider under its own terms.
- **Technical data.** IP addresses and request metadata are used for rate
  limiting and abuse prevention.

## 2. How we use your data

- To provide and operate the gateway and ad platform.
- To meter usage, apply the discount, and settle money transparently.
- To enforce rate limits, spend caps, and acceptable use.
- To prevent abuse and fraud.

We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 3. Inference providers and third parties

Your prompts are processed by the inference providers we route to. Their
handling of that data is governed by **their** privacy policies and terms, not
ours. If you require that prompts not be stored or used for training by a
provider, you are responsible for configuring that with the provider or choosing
a provider that guarantees it. **We do not use your prompts to train models.**

## 4. Sponsored content data

When a sponsor is shown, we record an impression and any click/attribution. This
is used to bill advertisers and to show them the performance of their offers.

## 5. Data retention

Ledger, account, and attribution records are retained to support billing,
settlement, and dispute resolution. We retain them as long as needed for these
purposes. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data
(see Contact).

## 6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR in the EU/EEA, CCPA in California),
you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and
to object to or restrict processing. Contact us to exercise these.

## 7. Security

We apply security controls including API-key authentication, magic-link sign-in,
rate limiting, spend caps, and validated advertiser URLs. No transmission or
storage is fully secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children

guac is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us
to remove it.

## 9. International data

Data may be processed in the US and other jurisdictions where our providers or
hosting (e.g. Fly.io) operate. By using guac you consent to this transfer.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will update the "Last updated" date;
continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
